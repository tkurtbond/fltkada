Package name
Fl_Rect FLTK.Rects

Note:

FLTK's Fl_Rect is all inline code, so FLTK.Rects is plain Ada, with nothing from the shim, and a Rect is a record with public components. Nothing is checked: a rectangle can be inset until its width or height is negative, as FLTK's can. Right and Bottom are the first coordinates outside the rectangle.
