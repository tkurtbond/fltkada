The samples of a binary PNM (P5, P6) with a maxval under 255 are scaled to 0 .. 255, as FLTK scales the text forms, and a sample over the maxval reads as 255. FLTK reads them as they are, so a P6 of pure red with maxval 1 was 1, 0, 0 (pofltk 45). A Shared_Image of one is scaled too, once Register_Images has been called.
