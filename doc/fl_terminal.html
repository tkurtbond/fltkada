       (X, Y, W, H : in Integer;
        Rows       : in Integer;
        Columns    : in Integer;
        History    : in Integer;
        Text       : in String := "")
    return Terminal;

Rows and Columns must be at least 1, History at least 0, and there must be no more than 2**24 cells; otherwise Constraint_Error is raised, rather than a failed allocation throwing through the shim. FLTK makes the history 100 rows whatever History says, so the cells are counted with at least that (pofltk 99).
