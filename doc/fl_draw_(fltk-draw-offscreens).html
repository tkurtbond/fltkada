Package name
fl_draw.H FLTK.Draw.Offscreens

Note:

An offscreen begun in a hook FLTK calls (a Draw, a callback) that raises before End_Drawing is ended as the exception leaves the hook. A Drawing_Guard begins its offscreen as it is declared, and ends it as it is finalized.
