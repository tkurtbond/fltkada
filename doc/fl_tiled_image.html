A copy of a tiled image whose colours have changed has its own copy of the tile, so it outlives the original. Otherwise the copy shares the tile, as in FLTK.

Both also override Image's Copy, so a Copy called on an Image'Class gives the same (FLTKAda-BUGS.md 101).
