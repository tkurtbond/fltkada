glut.H Binding Map

Back to Index

FLTK's GLUT emulation is bound in two packages: FLTK.GLUT holds the glut* functions, which refer to windows and menus by number as GLUT does, and FLTK.Widgets.Groups.Windows.OpenGL.GLUT holds Fl_Glut_Window. A window made with Create_Window or Create_Sub_Window has no Ada object; a Glut_Window made with Forge.Create is also a GLUT window and can be made current with Make_Current or Set_Window (Get_Number).
